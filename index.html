What I do

I work the alerts — and I write the detections that raise them.

Blue team security analyst and detection engineer focused on alert triage, incident investigation, and detection engineering across Windows/Sysmon event logs, Active Directory, network traffic, phishing artifacts, and AWS CloudTrail.

How I work an alert: scope before anything else (one host or many, one identity or many); look for what disproves me by writing down the benign hypothesis first; enrich identity, owner, change history, and reputation before escalating; deliver a verdict with an explicit confidence level; and treat every false positive as a tuning ticket.
